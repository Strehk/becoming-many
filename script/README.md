# Narration Script

The voiceover text of the experience, in both languages:

- [`en.md`](en.md) — English
- [`de.md`](de.md) — German

Both files carry the same nine sections in the same order:

| Section | EN | DE | Sense |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prologue | Prolog | — (the white void) |
| 2 | Chemical Sense — Scent | Chemischer Sinn – Geruch | scent |
| 3 | Bat — Depth | Fledermaus – Tiefe | echolocation |
| 4 | Insect — Motion | Insekt – Bewegung | motion vision |
| 5 | Infrared | Infrarot | thermal |
| 6 | Migratory Bird — The Magnetic Field | Zugvogel – Das Magnetfeld | magnetoreception |
| 7 | Finale | Finale | all layers at once |
| 8 | Overload | Überforderung | all layers, too much |
| 9 | Return | Rückkehr | layers stripped away |

## Notes

- The narration text itself is audience-facing experience content and therefore stays in its own
  language — this is the exception to the English-only rule in [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).
  Everything around it (filenames, headings of this index, commentary) is English.
- The two versions are not literal translations of one another. Where they diverge, both are
  intentional as written — do not "fix" one against the other.
- The section headings *Prologue / Prolog*, *Overload / Überforderung* and *Return / Rückkehr*
  were added for navigation; the delivered script left those three blocks untitled. All other
  headings are from the script as written.
