Becoming Many
Start Menu Study

Ten loading screens and start menus, from conventional to experimental, built from the particles of the piece's opening (White World, air motes, the teal flight path, dust-grain rings) and its finale (scent signatures, the connection web), set in Rubik like the end credits.

Every variant is fully working: loading, settings (saved across variants), credits, and the choice of desktop, VR headset or phone with real WebXR detection and motion-sensor access. Inside a variant: [ ] switch, R restart, H hide the switcher, Esc back. Add ?skip to jump past loading.
